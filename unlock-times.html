The “New Zealand trick” — what is actually true

Because the rollout follows local midnight, New Zealand reaches 19 November while it is still the morning of 18 November in the US and afternoon in India — Auckland's unlock (11:00 AM UTC on 18 Nov) comes about 13 hours before India's and 18 hours before New York's. Every launch week, the same idea spreads: make your console believe it is in New Zealand — on Xbox by changing the console's region setting, on PlayStation effectively only with a New Zealand PSN account — and the game unlocks at New Zealand's midnight instead of yours. It is widely reported by gaming press this week, and searches for it have surged.

What to weigh before trying it, stated neutrally:

  • It works, where it works, because the unlock checks the console/account region — it is not early access, and Rockstar's published schedule remains midnight local time on 19 November.
  • Region and account settings exist to match where you actually live and pay. Deliberately misrepresenting your region may conflict with the platform holders' terms of service, and store credit, payment methods and some content are region-locked — a PlayStation account's country cannot be changed after creation.
  • We describe the phenomenon because it is part of the launch story; we do not provide step-by-step instructions for circumventing a regional release schedule. If your region unlocks later, the preload is already on your console — it opens the moment your midnight arrives.
