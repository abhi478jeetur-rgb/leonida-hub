GTA 6 Price in India

GTA 6 costs ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition in India. Both prices apply on the PlayStation Store India and the Xbox Store India. Amazon India lists the boxed Standard Edition at ₹5,999 — note the box contains a download code, not a disc. In the US the game costs $79.99 (Standard) and $99.99 (Ultimate), so India's pricing is among the lowest in the world in dollar terms.

Which edition is better value?

For most players: Standard. Both editions are the same game with the same story and performance — the Ultimate Edition adds 16 in-game bonus items (vehicles, weapons, outfits, shops and activities listed on Rockstar's support page). Crucially, upgrading later costs the same total: the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is ₹1,500 on Xbox, exactly the gap between the editions, so buying Standard now does not lock you out or cost you extra. Buy Ultimate on day one only if you specifically want those items from the start. Full comparison: Standard vs Ultimate.

Either way, buy before 20 November 2026 and the digital editions include the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ under Rockstar's launch offer. Next steps: preload the game now and check when it unlocks for you.

Questions

How much is GTA 6 in India?

₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition, on both the PlayStation Store India and Xbox Store India. Amazon India lists the boxed Standard Edition at ₹5,999 (download code in a box). Checked 18 November 2026.

Can I upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later?

Yes — the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is ₹1,500 on Xbox India, exactly the price difference, so the total cost is the same. On PlayStation India the upgrade page showed “Not available for purchase” when checked on 16 November 2026.

Does the boxed copy contain a disc?

No. GTA 6 physical copies are code-in-a-box: you redeem the code and download the game (up to 212 GB). There is no physical Ultimate Edition.

Why do some sites show a different India price?

Older articles guessed prices before the store listings were confirmed, and console bundle prices (PS5 + game) are sometimes quoted as the game price. The figures on this page come from the live store listings as reported on the dates shown.
