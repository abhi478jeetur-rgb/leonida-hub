GTA 6 Editions: Standard vs Ultimate

Both editions contain the full GTA 6 game — identical story, world and performance. The Ultimate Edition (₹7,499 in India, $99.99 in the US) adds a bundle of 16 in-game bonus items for ₹1,500 / $20 more than Standard (₹5,999 / $79.99). And because the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately for exactly that difference (₹1,500 on Xbox India), buying Standard first and upgrading later costs the same total — there is no financial penalty for waiting.

What is confirmed in the Ultimate Edition

Rockstar's support page lists 16 items in the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, spanning extra vehicles, weapons, outfits, in-game shops and two activities, as checked in launch-week comparisons against the Rockstar, PlayStation and Xbox pages (16 November 2026). The items are in-game content and collectibles — no extra story missions and no performance advantage have been confirmed by Rockstar. Any website listing “exclusive story content” for Ultimate is going beyond the confirmed list; treat such claims as Unconfirmed until Rockstar publishes them.

The pre-order bonus both editions share

Buy any digital edition before 20 November 2026 and you also receive the Vintage Vice City Pack — a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and an exclusive weapon pattern — plus one month of GTA+. Claim windows differ by store (for example, PlayStation's terms ask you to claim the GTA+ month by 31 March 2027), so read your store's terms. The standalone Ultimate Edition Upgrade is not eligible for this offer — another reason the Standard-first route loses nothing.

Our verdict

  • Buy Standard if you just want the game — it is the complete game, and the upgrade stays available at the same total price.
  • Buy Ultimate if you are a collector-type player who will use the bonus vehicles, outfits and activities from hour one.
  • Either way, check the live store price, preload now, and know your unlock time.

A separate collector's box has been reported on sale in the US at $399.99, sold apart from the game itself Reported — it is not a third game edition and is not part of this comparison.
