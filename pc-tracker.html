GTA 6 PC Release Date Tracker

There is no GTA 6 PC release date, because Rockstar has not announced a PC version at all. GTA 6 launches 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and PC does not appear on Rockstar's official game page, in the pre-orders, or in Take-Two's fiscal 2027 release table. Everything else — including a reported Q1 2027 window — is labelled on this page as what it is: a report, not an announcement. This page is updated whenever Rockstar or Take-Two says something new.

A pattern is not a date. When Rockstar announces anything, it will appear on Rockstar's Newswire first, and this page will be updated the same day.

Questions

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not announced. Rockstar lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Take-Two's CEO calls PC a great, growing market and notes Rockstar titles historically reach other platforms over time — but no PC version, date or requirements are confirmed.

What is the GTA 6 PC release date?

There isn't one. GamesBeat's 13 Oct 2026 report (four anonymous sources) points to Q1 2027, unconfirmed — and Take-Two's release table lists GTA 6 console-only through 31 March 2027.

Are there official PC system requirements?

No — none can exist before a PC version is announced. Sites publishing “official GTA 6 PC requirements” are guessing; downloads claiming to be the PC game are scams.
