GTA 6 Download Time Calculator

GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space (install size reported from Rockstar's Newswire coverage; the exact figure varies by console). At 100 Mbps the download takes roughly 5 hours 15 minutes in real-world conditions; at 50 Mbps, about 10 hours 30 minutes. Enter your own speed below for your number — and start the preload well before the midnight unlock.

Default 212 GB is the reported maximum install size. Real-world efficiency is estimated at 90% of your rated speed (overhead, server load, Wi-Fi).

Will GTA 6 fit on your console?

Consoles reserve part of the drive for the system, so usable space is less than the number on the box. GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB free. Figures below are the widely reported usable capacities — check your own console's storage screen for the exact free space.

Tight on space? The fastest wins: move PS4/Xbox One games to an external USB drive, delete game capture clips, and remember a PS5 system update (~1.2 GB) is also required before GTA 6 will launch. Full steps in the preload guide.

How the maths works: download time = size in gigabits ÷ speed in Mbps. 212 GB ≈ 1,696 gigabits (using 1 GB = 8 gigabits), so at 100 Mbps the ideal time is 16,960 seconds ≈ 4 h 43 m. Consoles also install while downloading, which can add time on slower connections.
