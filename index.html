The short version

GTA 6 releases on Thursday, 19 November 2026 at 12:00 AM local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. In India it costs ₹5,999 (Standard) and ₹7,499 (Ultimate) on both console stores. The download needs up to 212 GB of free space, preload has been open since 10 November, and there is no PC version announced. Buying any digital edition before 20 November 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+.
